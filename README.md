# HandsMen Threads – Salesforce Fashion Management Platform

HandsMen Threads is a Salesforce-based premium fashion management platform designed to streamline **men’s bespoke tailoring**, improve **customer experience**, and automate key business operations.

The project focuses on developing a robust Salesforce data model to efficiently manage customers, products, orders, inventory, and loyalty information while maintaining **data accuracy and integrity directly through the user interface**.

## Key Features

* **Customer & Order Management** – Manage customer information, products, tailoring orders, and order details through a centralized Salesforce platform.
* **Automated Order Confirmations** – Automatically send email confirmations to customers after an order is successfully placed.
* **Dynamic Loyalty Program** – Automatically update customer loyalty status based on purchase history and spending, enabling personalized rewards and better customer engagement.
* **Proactive Stock Alerts** – Automatically notify the warehouse team through email when product stock falls below five units to support timely restocking.
* **Scheduled Bulk Order Processing** – Process bulk orders automatically at midnight, updating financial records and adjusting inventory levels for accurate daily operations.
* **Data Quality & Integrity** – Implement validation rules and automation to ensure accurate, consistent, and reliable business data.
* **Automated Business Workflows** – Reduce manual work by integrating Salesforce Flows and Apex-based automation into business processes.

## Salesforce Technologies Used

* Data Modeling
* Data Validation & Data Quality
* Lightning App Builder
* Record-Triggered Flows
* Apex
* Apex Triggers
* Asynchronous Apex
* Email Automation
* Scheduled Automation

## Learning Outcomes

Through this project, I gained practical experience in designing Salesforce data models, building Lightning applications, implementing declarative automation with Flows, developing Apex and Triggers, and handling asynchronous business processes.

The project demonstrates how Salesforce can be used to build a scalable fashion business platform that connects **customer management, order processing, inventory control, loyalty programs, and business automation** in one system.
